\# Existing E-Governance Systems Review



\## Purpose of the Review



Before deciding the requirements of our system, it is important to look at existing e-governance and citizen service platforms. The purpose of this review is to understand how real systems allow citizens to report problems, how service requests are managed, and which features could be useful for our proposed system. This will also help us avoid adding features without a practical reason.



\## Open311



Open311 is an open standard used for reporting non-emergency civic problems. It supports location-based service requests for issues such as potholes, street cleaning, graffiti, and other local problems. A request can contain information about the type of issue, its location, a description, and other supporting details.



One useful idea from Open311 is that civic complaints can follow a common and structured format instead of being submitted as unorganized messages. This can make it easier for a city system to receive requests and send them to the appropriate service or department.



For our project, the main lesson from Open311 is the importance of structured complaint information, especially the issue category, description, location, and a unique request ID.
