\# System Constraints and Considerations



\## System Constraints



The proposed system may face some practical limitations during development and real-world use.



\* Users need an internet connection to submit and track service requests.

\* Some citizens may not have smartphones or may have limited digital skills.

\* Location information may not always be accurate.

\* Uploaded photos may be unclear or may not fully show the reported problem.

\* Different municipal departments may follow different service procedures.

\* Resolution time may depend on available staff, equipment, weather, and other resources.

\* The first version of the system will focus on selected municipal services rather than every possible government service.



\## Assumptions



For the initial system plan, we assume that:



\* Citizens have access to a phone number or email for account and notification purposes.

\* Municipal departments have authorized staff who can review and update service requests.

\* Each service category can be connected to a responsible department.

\* Officers will update request progress when important actions take place.

\* Citizens will provide correct information when submitting a request.

\* The municipality will define expected resolution times for different service categories.



\## Privacy and Regulatory Considerations



The system may collect personal information, complaint details, photographs, and location data. This information should only be collected when necessary and should not be exposed publicly without a valid reason.



Access to citizen information should depend on the user's role. For example, an officer may need access to information related to an assigned request, while unrelated users should not be able to see private citizen details.



The system should also keep records of important actions such as request assignment, status changes, and resolution updates. These records can help maintain accountability and support future review of a complaint.



Before real deployment, the system would also need to follow applicable government policies, data-protection requirements, accessibility guidelines, and municipal procedures.
